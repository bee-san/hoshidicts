# Georgian (`ka`)

Deinflection rules, text processors and scanning settings for Georgian in the
Mkhedruli script. Lookups start at the hovered word and try only prefixes that
end at a word boundary (`word` / `word` / `spaces`, docs/languages.md 14.1).
Hyphens and spaces separate words, so a phrase the dictionary has is found
over two words (დაბადების დღეზე → დაბადების დღე). The rules reach a noun or
adjective in the nominative singular and a verb in the third person singular
of the present (წერს) or, for a perfective verb, of the future (დაწერს), the
forms English Wiktionary lists them under.

The recommended dictionary, wty-ka-en, tags no row with a part of speech, so
the pack sets `lookup.untaggedTerms` to `pass` (docs/languages.md 4.4, 9.4).
Its rules keep their conditions: noun outputs `nom`, verb outputs `v`, and
intermediate states that no dictionary term matches.

## Rule provenance

- **Final set**: `transforms.json` is `sources/own.json`: 56 transforms and
  1,185 rules (552 `suffix`, 301 `prefix`, 88 `circumfix` rows, 244
  `wholeWord`), all `own`. No rule is `both`: Yomitan's rules strip an
  ending to the bare stem (სახლში → სახლ), which no own rule does.
- **Own rules** (commit `Georgian (ka): own rules`, written before Yomitan's
  file was read):
  - nouns and adjectives: ergative, dative, genitive, instrumental,
    adverbial and vocative for consonant stems, truncating -ა/-ე stems and
    -ო/-უ stems; syncope as a step of its own (წყლის → წყალის → წყალი), so a
    reading without it has the shorter trace; the -ებ- plural and the
    archaic plural in -ნ-/-თ-; the -ა extension (სახლისა); the postpositions
    -ში, -ზე, -თან, -ვით, -თვის, -გან, -კენ, -ადმი, -ებრ, -დან, -ურთ, -მდე on
    their case forms; the superlative უ-...-ესი, the moderative მო-...-ო and
    the attributive form without -ი;
  - clitics: -ც, -ვე, -ღა, the copula -ა/-აა, the quotatives -ო, -მეთქი,
    -თქო;
  - pronouns: whole-word rules for the oblique forms of the personal,
    demonstrative and interrogative pronouns (ჩემთვის → მე, ამისი → ეს);
  - verbs: person and number endings of the present and future, imperfect
    and conditional, subjunctives; aorist and optative (weak, strong with
    the root vowel, -ევ verbs, passives in -ება/-დება); the perfect series
    through its inversion markers (დამიწერია → დაუწერია → დაწერს); the
    perfect of intransitives through the participle; the person markers
    ვ-, მ-, გ-, გვ- (with მი-/გი-/გვი- for უ- and the third-person ს-/ჰ-)
    after any of 19 preverbs; preverb removal from the future to the
    present, and the medial future with ი- and -ებ- (ითამაშებს →
    თამაშობს).
- **Changes after Yomitan's file was read**: the conditions both files have
  (`v`, `n`, `adj`, `adv`) got Yomitan's definitions, as
  `tools/merge-transforms.mjs` requires, and the adverbial state became
  `advc`; the 72,062 UniMorph forms gave the same 3,847,353 deinflections
  (1,966,457 distinct texts) before and after. Later changes came from
  measurements:
  - the verb rules alternated two conditions (`vs` after an ending rule,
    `vp` after a prefix rule), so no ending rule read its own output. The
    first version had one condition, and two aorist rules (-ა → -ება, -ი →
    -ება) fed each other until `hoshidicts-cli deinflect` crashed (the
    growing loop hachi-lang-la reported). The alternation did not bound a
    prefix rule and an ending rule taking turns; the third state of the
    audit fixes below does;
  - fixes from the two books and the frequent words: -ა/-ე stems after a
    vowel (ავარიის → ავარია); no aorist -ა after -ებ-/-ობ- (გაკეთება is a
    verbal noun); a preverb that starts a longer one is not removed where
    the longer one fits (გადარჩება → რჩება); person prefixes need four
    letters after them without a preverb and five after ა- (ვისი, მისი,
    მათ, ამისი); syncope only feeds -ი and -ა stems (მესმის is not მესამე);
    the medial future needs a stem of three letters (იაგო, a name, is not
    read as აგებს); the verbs of motion take -ვარ, -ხარ (მიდიხარ → მიდის);
  - forms the own rules missed: the ს-/ჰ- of a third-person indirect object
    in `pres-1` (ვთხოვ → სთხოვს); `obj-2`'s გა- before a stem in ა-
    (გაკეთებს → აკეთებს); the version vowel უ- in the perfect, pluperfect
    and perfect subjunctive (დაუბრუნებია → დააბრუნებს); the participles in
    -ენილი, -ებული, -ილი made into a future in -ება; the aorists -ო → -ოფს
    and -ბა → -ბ- plus the thematic suffix, and the optative -აო → -ავებს;
    the pronoun genitives იმისი, ამისი, მაგისი;
  - stems may hold hyphens everywhere and spaces in the noun, clitic and
    verb-ending rules, so phrases inflect on their last word; the genitive
    stays one word, so როგორ არის is not read as the phrase როგორ არა.

- **Preverbs that change the meaning.** Removing the preverb (future →
  present) also reaches a different verb where the preverb is lexical:
  გასცემს "gives away" → სცემს "beats", დაშლა → შლის, მოიცავს "includes"
  → იცავს "protects". 10 of the 47 wrong first results of the frequent
  words, Tatoeba (without the name Tom), the books and the first auditor's
  texts that are not own entries have a `pv` step, and so do 7 of the 8
  wrong frequent words the measurement does not judge.
- **Quotative after a vowel.** A quotative -ო after a vowel shows the
  vocative step where it is not a vocative: 55 first results in Tatoeba,
  the books and the first auditor's texts, and 43 in the second's. In 30
  the stem is not a noun (რაო → რა 16 times, არაო, სადაო, უნდაო); in 25 the
  vocative step comes before a case or verb form, which has no vocative
  (სოფელსაო → სოფელი, ქმარსაო, იმედითაო, ავაო → ადის). The lemma is right;
  Georgian also has real vocatives in -ო after a vowel (ძმაო, მამაო, დაო),
  which the same rule reads, and wty-ka-en's untagged rows cannot tell a
  noun from another word.
- **Bare second person of root verbs.** A root verb's second person
  without ვ- (ჩან, ხვეწ, თხოვ, ითხოვ) finds nothing or a noun: 4 positions
  in Tatoeba, the books and the first auditor's texts. A rule adding -ს to
  every word that ends in a consonant would reach them and many false
  candidates.
- **Old scripts.** Asomtavruli and Nuskhuri (Khutsuri) are only lower-cased,
  not converted to Mkhedruli, so a Khutsuri text finds nothing in a
  Mkhedruli dictionary.
- **Form-of rows of pronouns.** wty-ka-en's pronoun tables list every
  personal pronoun as a form of every other one (მე is a row naming შენ, ის,
  ჩვენ ...). Each of the 35 forms whose rows name six or more pronouns
  shows the right pronoun first, through the `pron` transform or its own
  entry (მის → ის, იგინი → იგი; შენით → შენი is ambiguous); the other
  pronouns follow it.
- **The frequency list** counts the image-link keyword მინი "thumb" of
  the dump's markup as a word (36,457 tokens), because `wiki-text.py`'s
  cleaning keeps the text after a link's first `|`. It is labelled X and
  not judged.
