# Arabic (prototype pack)

A prototype pack that proves the language framework end to end for
Hachidori's prototype: right-to-left text, a joined script, a pack
setting with a display effect, and Yomitan's 7,157 Arabic rules. It lives
on the `prototype/en-ar` branch only and is never merged: the real Arabic
pack is written later, from scratch, by its own language agent.

## Rule provenance

- Yomitan (`source: "yomitan"`): 50 transforms, 7,157 rules, none left out.
  - 537 literal prefix and suffix rules, ported as they are.
  - 50 rules with a lookahead or lookbehind next to the affix
    (`conditionalPrefixInflection`, `conditionalSuffixInflection`):
    `^لل(?!ل)` is a `prefix` with `stemNotStartsWith: [ل]`, `(?<!ن)ن$` a
    `suffix` with `stemNotEndsWith: [ن]`.
  - 6,570 prefix + stem + suffix rules (`sandwichInflection`, generated by
    `getImperfectRules`): `circumfix` rules with `stemCharacters: {letter}`
    (Yomitan's `arabicLetters`, U+064B-U+065F included) and
    `stemMinLength: 1`, plus `stemNotEndsWith: [ن]` where Yomitan has
    `(?<!ن)`. They are written as 21 cross-product rows, one per set of
    prefixes that share their suffixes within a transform; the mapping
    script checks that the rows expand to exactly the 6,570 rules.
- Own (`source: "own"`): 2 transforms, 30 rules, applied to verb stems:
  `VDoubled` (28 rules: a final doubled consonant collapses, `مددت` → `مد`,
  `استحققن` → `استحق`) and `VFinalWeak` (final `ي` → `ى` and `و` → `ا`,
  `رميت` → `رمى`, `دعوت` → `دعا`).
- No rule is `both`.

## Measurements

`hoshidicts-cli eval-language` from feat/multi-language at 1faeed5 (GCC
14.3, Release), with `--dictionary` Wiktionary Arabic-English
(wiktionary-to-yomitan 2026.10.22, `wty-ar-en.zip`, sha256
`cf45a3a3…fd47`, CC BY-SA 4.0). The sample is the 2,000 UniMorph rows of
`corpus.tsv` and `gaps.tsv` together.

| Own (`sources/own.json`) | 17.9% (358) | 299 / 28 / 31 | 0.861 | 17.7% | 5 / 14 | 4 / 4 | 0 / 261, negatives 121 / 121 |
| Yomitan (`sources/yomitan.json`) | 77.15% (1,543) | 769 / 255 / 519 | 2.452 | 17.8% | 10 / 14 | 4 / 4 | 261 / 261, negatives 121 / 121 |

- The own rules alone reach 358 rows mostly through the processors:
  UniMorph's vocalised source becomes the lemma once its vowel marks are
  removed (an indefinite singular noun).
- First rank is low because the dictionary lists inflected forms as
  headwords of their own (`يكتبون`, with "form of" glosses), so a lookup of
  the form ranks that entry first.
- `--per-rule` on the final set: 48 of the 638 rule rows recover sampled
  rows; 45 recover none and add 227 false candidates in all (mostly
  imperfect-prefix, imperative and 4 `VDoubled` rules); no rule breaks a
  negative. All are kept for the prototype.
- `tools/check-yomitan.mjs` against the same CLI: 10,585 inputs, 150,347
  Yomitan results, 692 differences, every one an empty text the engine does
  not return (listed in `sources/yomitan-differences.tsv`); the six
  processors agree with Yomitan's on 2,412 to 3,206 inputs each.
- `tests/lookup.tsv` (11 rows) passes against `tests/dictionary/` imported
  by `hoshidicts-cli import` and looked up with `hoshidicts-cli lookup`.
- Lookup speed is not measured: `benchmark-lookup` has no language option
  at 1faeed5.

## Known gaps

- Broken plurals (`شبان` → `شاب`, `الأمراء` → `أمير`, `التجارب` →
  `تجربة`): no affix rule reaches them; they are most noun misses.
- Hollow and other weak verbs whose stem loses or changes a radical
  (`تغبن` → `غاب`, `تسقوا` → `أسقى`).
- UniMorph's own spelling: some subjunctive and jussive plurals lack the
  final alif (`يسخرو`), and some lemmas lack a hamza their forms have
  (`اباحي`, forms `إباحي…`).
- `VFinalWeak` also applies to the scanned text itself, so any word ending
  in `ي` or `و` gets a candidate ending in `ى` or `ا`; the dictionary query
  and the part-of-speech filter remove those that are not verbs.
