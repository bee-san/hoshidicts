# Albanian (`sq`)

Deinflection rules, text processors and scanning settings for Albanian
(standard, Tosk-based spelling). Lookups start at the hovered word and try
only prefixes that end at a word boundary (`word` / `word` / `spaces`,
docs/languages.md 14.1). An apostrophe or hyphen ends a word, so *ministri*
in *ish-ministri* and *kam* in *s’kam* can be looked up, and the rules read
the contractions *s’* and *ç’* (`s’kam` → `kam`). The analytic tenses are
read from their first word, as the dictionary's own form-of rows read them
(`kam punuar`, `do të shkoj`, `u largua`, `duke ecur` → `punoj`, `shkoj`,
`largohem`, `eci`), and so is an adjective with its article (`e madhe`,
`më i mirë` → `madh`, `mirë`).

## Rule provenance

`transforms.json` has 150 transforms and 2,078 rules (2,354 with the
circumfix cross products expanded): 1,935 `own`, 143 `both` and no
`yomitan` rule. `sources/build-final.mjs` writes it: `sources/own.json` with
every Yomitan rule left out and the own rules that make a Yomitan rule's
deinflection marked `both`.

- **`both`**: an own rule of the same type in the transform of the same
  form that replaces the same ending with the same text, or a longer ending
  with a common part in front (Yomitan's aorist `va` → `j` is the own `ova` →
  `oj`, `eva` → `ej`, `iva` → `ij`, … on the stems Yomitan's rule reaches).
  91 of Yomitan's 94 rules have such own rules
  (`sources/yomitan-provenance.tsv` lists them). The other three are
  Yomitan's nominalization (`im` → `oj`, `im` → `ej`, `je` → ``).
- **Yomitan rules left out**: all 94. On 117,771 words (the frequency
  list, the single-word forms of UniMorph sqi and of the dictionary's
  form-of rows; `sources/measure.py yomitan`, which also prints a table of
  the per-rule counts and examples), each Yomitan rule
  applied alone gives a headword with a compatible part of speech that no
  final deinflection gives 788 times (798 before nxë and the compounds of
  vë and zë were added, 767 before the active verbs in -gj lost their twin
  rules, Known gaps). A random sample of 50 of the 798, judged by hand: 8 right
  (`lavdin` → `lavdi`, `fea` → `fe`, `magjin` → `magji`, `selin` → `seli`,
  `shtetast` → `shtetas`, `oficerja` → `oficer`, `nxëmë` → `nxë`, `përzëmë`
  → `përzë`), 3 feminine nouns of persons read as the masculine (`mikja` →
  `mik`, `nxënësja` → `nxënës`, `përkthyesja` → `përkthyes`, whose rows
  name the feminine), and 39 wrong. The wrong ones come from Yomitan's rules having no minimum stem
  or class (`deti` → `de`, `arta` → `ar`, `ma` → `më`, `liqen` → `liq`,
  `drejtorë` → `drejtoj` through `rë` → `j`, `botë` → `boj` through `të` →
  `j`), and from spellings that are not words (`përfundojni`, `irnojnin`).
  The last two right ones led to own rules for nxë and the compounds of vë
  and zë. An own rule for the feminine nouns of
  persons (`aktore` → `aktor` after `-ja` → `-e`) was tried and removed: on
  the Wikipedia text it gave 16 new first results, 13 of them wrong
  (`terren` → `terr`, `shtatorja` → `shtator`).
- **Own rules**: tenses and persons are separate transforms, so a trace
  names both (`aorist (3sg)`, `non-active present (3sg)`, `perfect (1pl)`):
  - nouns: definite, oblique and plural endings with the masculine and
    feminine allomorphs (`mali`, `miku`, `syri`, `babai`, `vëllanë`;
    `vajza`, `lulja`, `pyetja`, `shtëpia`, `kalaja`, `shtëpisë`, `vajze`,
    `luleje`), the loss of unstressed ë (`libri`, `motrës`, `ëndrrat`,
    `organizmi`), the plural types (`male`, `shokë`, `gishta`, `libra`,
    `miq`, `zogj`, `parqe`, `lumenj`, `shembuj`, `qiej`, `fshatra`,
    `ujëra`, `mbretër`, `baballarë`, `vëllezër`) and the irregular plurals
    (`djem`, `kuaj`, `duar`, `dyer`, `net`, `gra`, `njerëz`);
  - adjectives: the article (`i`, `e`, `të`, `së`), the comparative `më`,
    feminine, plural and definite forms (`e madhe`, `të mëdha`, `të
    mëdhenj`, `e vogël`, `të vogla`, `të vegjël`, `e re`, `të rinj`, `i
    miri`, `e mira`, `të mirët`);
  - verbs: present, imperfect, aorist (by class: `punova`, `shkrova`,
    `ktheva`, `theva`, `bleva`, `fshiva`, `hyra`, `lava`, `mbajta`, `hapa`,
    `preku`, `pyeta`), subjunctive, optative, admirative (through the
    participle: `punuakam`, `hapka`, `qenka`), imperative, participle, the
    non-active present, imperfect, imperative and aorist (to the active verb
    and to the mediopassive verb a dictionary lists: `largohet` →
    `largohem`, `largoj`), the analytic tenses (`do të`, `të`, `kam`,
    `kisha`, `pata`, `jam`, `isha`, `paça`, `paskam`, `paskësha`, `qenkam`,
    `qenkësha`, `qofsha`, `u`, `duke`, `pa`, `për të`), the pronouns an
    imperative takes (`hape`, `lexoje`, `tregoji`, `jepmë`, `tregona`,
    `hapeni`), verbal nouns in -im (`bashkim` → `bashkoj`), and the
    irregular verbs: jam, kam, them, jap, shoh, vij, dua, ha, pi, di, rri,
    fle, lë, vë, zë, nxë, bie, bëj, dal, marr, flas, pres, vras, shes, pyes,
    njoh, vdes, godas, humbas, stërvis, gjej, ngre, blej, vete, iki, eci,
    hipi, arrij, mbërrij and the ablaut verbs hedh, heq, zgjedh, mbledh,
    sjell, mbjell, vjell, djeg, ndjek, pjek, nxjerr, bredh, vjedh, pjell,
    mjel, vjel, tjerr, ndez, vesh, rrah, whose forms of four letters or more
    are suffix rules so that compounds share them (`tërhoqi` → `tërheq`).
- **Stem constraints** keep the rules off the words they would misread:
  - every suffix rule needs a stem without a space, so the last word of a
    phrase is never read on its own (`e ka`, `të mira`);
  - minimum stems and final-letter classes: `hej` is not `hell`, `pra` is
    not `pre`, `lart` is not `lar`, `mira` and `mia` are not `mi`, `vetmi` is not
    `vetëm`, `hyjni` is not `hyj` (a verb stem before a consonant ending
    does not end in j; the non-active endings have twin rules for the
    digraphs gj and nj, so `përgjigjet` → `përgjigjem`);
  - the one-syllable verbs (jam, kam, ha, pi, di, rri, fle, lë, vë, zë,
    nxë, bie, dua, vete) have whole-word forms, so `dalësh` is not da- +
    `lësh`;
  - after an auxiliary only the participle rules continue (`për të marrë`
    → `marr`, not the adjective `i marrë`), and after `të` the oblique
    definite adjective is read only in -s and -it after `e`, `së` and `i`,
    and in -it only for adjectives in -r, since after `të` these endings are
    genitive nouns (`të fizikës`, `të lumit`);
  - the mediopassive lemma of the non-active aorist, perfect and gerund is
    read only after `u` and the non-active auxiliaries (circumfix rules:
    `u largova`, `është ulur`, `duke u larë`), not after `ka` (`ka ardhur`
    is not `ardhem`).
- **History**: the first commit of this pack, `sq: own rules`, has the own
  rules as written before Yomitan's file was read (149 transforms, 1,848
  rules). Later commits changed them after measuring:
  - the conditions both files have took Yomitan's definitions (`v`; `n`
    with `np` and `ns`; `adj`; `adv`), which the merge tool requires; the
    intermediate states (`v_sbjv`, `v_aor`, `v_imp`, `v_ptcp`, `adj_m`,
    `adj_f`, `adj_pl`, `adj_cmp`) keep their own names;
  - after reading Yomitan's rules: consonant-final feminine nouns
    (`fluturën`), masculines in -ua (`dragoi` → `dragua`), `zë` → `zëri`,
    -o plurals (`pianot`), the non-active aorist stem alone (`kthye`,
    `largua`), the verbal-noun transform with a minimum stem, plural case
    endings that may leave the singular (`pijet` → `pije`), and nxë with the
    compounds of vë and zë;
  - after the frequent-word, Tatoeba and real-text checks below: the stem
    constraints above, the mediopassive verb before the active one, one-step
    readings of irregular imperatives with a pronoun (`lëre` → `lë`, `jepi`
    → `jap`), and `mos u shqetëso` → `shqetësohem`;
  - after the mutation check (docs/languages.md 14 step 7): a test for every
    constraint, and prefix rules without a minimum stem (which changed
    nothing).

`lookup.formOf` `follow`. The sample is the 1,000 UniMorph rows of
`corpus.tsv` and `gaps.tsv` together. The pack's one optional feature is the
`addMissingDiacritics` processor (the `missingDiacritics` setting); each
file is measured with every option on and with the defaults.

## Known gaps

- `gaps.tsv`: three UniMorph rows give iki a vowel stem (`ikinim`,
  `ikikeni`, `ikiët`), two are misspellings (`thithëës`, `mendjve`), and
  `qenkëshe arratisuar` → `arratisem` has a participle in -uar for a verb in
  -isem.
- The dictionary's own form-of rows decide many ties: `luanin` (they
  played) shows `luan` (lion) first, because wty-sq-en lists `luanin` as
  the definite accusative of `luan` and has no table for luaj.
- Ties between readings of equal length and steps that no form-of row
  decides follow rule order, nouns before adjectives before verbs: `dalin`
  → `dalë` before `dal`, `huaja` → `hua` before `huaj`. Verbs, then
  adjectives, then nouns change 102 first results of the Wikipedia text (19
  distinct): by my reading 48 get better (`huaja`, `të mundur`, `dalin`)
  and 35 worse (`toka` → `tok` instead of `tokë`, `të tretë`), so the order
  stays.
- Words the dictionary lacks are read as the nearest form the rules reach:
  feminine nouns of persons as the masculine is not done (see Rule
  provenance), so `aktorja` finds nothing when the dictionary has only
  `aktor`; `telefonatë` → `telefon`, `hekurt` (`i hekurt`, of iron) →
  `hekur`.
- Informal spelling without ë and ç is read only with the
  `missingDiacritics` setting. Without it, frequent subtitle words give
  another headword first: `jane` → `jan` (2,968 occurrences in the list),
  `ben` → `be` (2,093), `bere` → `ber` (1,720), `thash` → `thaj` (1,125),
  `kan` → `ka` (915), `beje` → `be` (895). A dropped final ë (`pun` for
  `punë`) is not read.
- Abstract nouns in -i that the dictionary lacks are read as the
  definite adjective they derive from: `çmenduri` (madness, 876
  occurrences) → `çmendur` (mad).
- The few active verbs whose stem ends in the digraph gj or nj (dergj,
  gjegj, regj) are not read in their consonant-ending forms (`regjin`); the
  non-active ones (përgjigjem, shmenjem) are.
- Possessives with their article (`i imi`, `e mia`, `të mitë`) are read as
  adjectives (`i imi` → `imtë`).
- The pronouns before a verb (`e di`, `më thuaj`, `ma jep`, `ju lutem`) are
  not removed: a hover on the pronoun shows the pronoun.
- Gheg forms (`me punue`, `âsht`, `kam punue`) are not deinflected; the
  dictionary's own Gheg form-of rows are followed.
- The non-active forms read in one step by the mediopassive circumfix
  (`u largova` → `largohem`) have the trace `non-active`, without tense
  and person; the same forms read as the active verb have both.
- `tests/lookup.tsv` checks the first result's expression and matched
  text, not its trace, so no lookup test observes `traceSelection`; it is
  set as the contract recommends.
