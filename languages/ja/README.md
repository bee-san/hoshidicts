# Japanese (`ja`)

The Japanese pack is today's Japanese pipeline written as data: the
deinflection rules, conditions and text processors hoshidicts compiled in
until 35e2dab. It is built into the library (`src/languages/builtin_packs.cpp`)
and must give exactly the results the old code gives; the parity tests compare
it with that code, kept in `tests/reference/` (`docs/languages.md`, sections
11 and 11.3).

## Rule provenance

- 75 transforms: 57 with `source: "yomitan"` (every Japanese transform of
  Yomitan at d3c54c1) and 18 with `source: "own"`, the hoshidicts auxiliary
  groups Yomitan lacks: `-ましゅ`, `-ください`, `-くださる`, `-ごらん`,
  `-ごらんなさい`, `-いただく`, `-あげる`, `-くれる`, `-もらう`, `-やる`,
  `-さしあげる`, `-みる`, `-みせる`, `-ある`, `-いく`, `-くる`, `-なさそう`,
  `-ながら`.
- 983 rules (907 written in `src/deinflector.cpp`, 76 generated by its
  `add_irregular` for the irregular verbs いく/行く/逝く/往く, the godan
  う verbs こう, とう, 請う, ... and のたまう, たまう, たゆたう), in the order the
  deinflector added them: 975 `suffix` and 8 `wholeWord`.
- 54 rules belong to the 18 `own` transforms. Two more are `own` inside
  Yomitan's `imperative`: `ませ` → `ます` and `くれ` → `くれる`.
- The other 927 rules are Yomitan's 928 at d3c54c1; Yomitan lists `-え`
  `れえ` → `らい` twice, and the pack once, as hoshidicts always did.
- 22 conditions in the bit order of today's `Conditions` enum: the 18 flags
  `v1d`, `v1p`, `v5d`, `v5ss`, `v5sp`, `vk`, `vs`, `vz`, `adj-i`, `-ます`,
  `-ません`, `-て`, `-ば`, `-く`, `-た`, `-ん`, `-なさい`, `-ゃ`, then `v1`,
  `v5s`, `v5` and `v` with `subConditions`. Keys are Yomitan's. Exactly the six
  tags today's `pos_to_conditions` maps (`v1`, `v5`, `vk`, `vs`, `vz`, `adj-i`)
  have `isDictionaryForm: true`.

## Measurements

- `tests/yomitan.tsv`: all 1,446 rows pass (1,428 distinct sources, 1,382 of
  them in `valid = 1` rows, the word list of the benchmarks); the parity
  tests require identical deinflections, processor variants and lookups for
  every row, every prefix of every source, deep chains and 100,000 generated
  strings.
- `tests/cases.tsv`: 46 rows pass.
- `hoshidicts-cli eval-language` and the lookup timings against Jitendex are
  recorded in `docs/benchmarks-languages.md`.

## Known gaps

- Romaji input (Yomitan's `alphabeticToHiragana`) is not offered, as before.
- Yomitan's processors `normalizeCombiningCharacters`,
  `normalizeCJKCompatibilityCharacters`, `normalizeRadicalCharacters` and
  `alphanumericWidthVariants` (half-width direction) are not ported one by one:
  `ja.nfkc` and `ja.fullwidthAlphanumeric` cover them as hoshidicts always
  did. The pack is exempt from the processor comparison of
  `tools/check-yomitan.mjs` (`docs/languages.md` 8.2); its processors are
  proved against the old code instead.
- `traceSelection` is `first`: a term keeps the first deinflection that reached
  it at its longest matched length, as before, where Yomitan keeps the
  shortest.
- `formOf` is `asTerm`: a row whose definitions are `[term, [rules]]` form-of
  references is an ordinary term with its glossary as stored, as before, where
  Yomitan follows the references (`docs/languages.md` 9.10). Jitendex has
  138,071 such rows (of 436,555), each a visible "⟶" redirect glossary beside
  one reference. Switching to `follow` is the owner's decision; it would also
  break ties every ranking key leaves by discovery order (9.6).
