# Modern Greek (`el`)

Deinflection rules, text processors and scanning settings for Standard
Modern Greek in the monotonic spelling; polytonic and older text is read as
well. Lookups start at the hovered word and try only prefixes that end at a
word boundary (`word` / `word` / `spaces`, docs/languages.md 14.1). The
apostrophe of an elided word belongs to the word (`απ'`, `'ρθει`,
`σ'αγαπώ`).

## Rule provenance

`transforms.json` is the union of `sources/own.json` and
`sources/yomitan.json` written by `node tools/merge-transforms.mjs el
--force`; no rule was removed from it. It has 78 transforms and 1,078 rule
entries, 6,078 rules with the cross products expanded: 6,072 `own`, 6
`yomitan`, none `both`.

- **Yomitan's rules**: the transform `ξανα-` (six prefix rules). All six are
  kept. Four of the seven rows of Yomitan's test are reached only by them
  (`ξανανθίζω` → ανθίζω, `ξανάβαλε` → έβαλε, `ξανάρχισε` → άρχισε, `ξανάπα`
  → είπα); two of those four rules add one false candidate each, and none
  breaks a negative (`--per-rule`). The other two give readings the own
  rules give too. The own transform shares their id; its three rules differ from
  Yomitan's in their stem constraints and in giving the stem to the verb
  rules (`ξανάγραψα` → γράψα → γράφω; Yomitan stops at the prefix), so the
  merge tool counts none of them as `both`.
- **Own rules**, by transform (the trace names tense and person or case and
  number: `aorist (1st pl)`, `genitive plural`):
  - conjugation I (γράφω, γράφομαι) and II (αγαπάω/αγαπώ, θεωρώ; αγαπιέμαι,
    κοιμάμαι, θεωρούμαι), active and mediopassive, in the present,
    imperfect, aorist, perfective non-past, imperatives, gerund and
    participle; the augment έ-/ή- and the older ε- before a stressed stem
    (εφώναξε), the internal augment of learned compounds (επέστρεψα →
    επιστρέφω, συνέλαβε → συλλαμβάνω), the movable -ν (είπεν);
  - the perfective stems: -σ-, -ψ-, -ξ- with the consonant they replace, the
    asigmatic stems (έφερα, έστειλα, πέθανα) and the irregular stems
    compounds share (έφυγα, έμεινα, κατάλαβα, έδωσα); the passive stems -τ-,
    -θ-, -στ-, -χτ-, -φτ-, -ηθ- and the learned -θη, -ησαν;
  - irregular verbs written out (είμαι, έχω, λέω, πάω/πηγαίνω, τρώω, βλέπω,
    βρίσκω, μπαίνω, βγαίνω, παίρνω, πίνω, έρχομαι, γίνομαι, ζω, ακούω, …);
  - the declensions of nouns and adjectives (-ος, -ο, -ι, -μα, -ος/-η, -ας,
    -ης, -ές, -ούς, -έας, -α, -η/-εις, -ού, -ον, -ων/-ουσα, -ύς, -ής), with
    names and nationality nouns, which may start with a capital, in their
    case endings only; comparatives and superlatives, participles and
    diminutives as chains from the masculine an adjective rule makes
    (ψηλότερης → ψηλότερος → ψηλός);
  - elision and aphaeresis (`απ'`, `κ'` → και, `'ρθει` → έρθει, `'ς` →
    εις), elided words written together with the next one (`σ'αγαπώ`), and
    one-syllable words written with an accent (`μιά`, `ποιός`);
  - **the accent**. Greek moves the accent when an ending adds or removes a
    syllable (αγόρασα, αγοράζω; άνθρωπος, ανθρώπου). A rule whose ending
    moves it writes the lemma's ending and an internal condition saying
    where the accent goes (`v_acc`, `n_last`, `n_ante`, `adj_last`,
    `adj_ante`), and the transform `accent` puts it there with a stem change
    (one rule per vowel letter). Such a reading has two trace steps:
    `aorist (1st sg)`, then `accent`. Rules with `pattern` steps would have
    given one step, but `tests/classes.json` records every such step on
    every corpus string (docs/languages.md 12.1), which made it 4.8 MB.
- **Conditions**: verb rules write `v`, noun rules `n`, adjective rules
  `adj`; `aug` marks a past form whose internal augment was removed, which
  only verb rules take. A rule of a declension takes only the text as
  scanned, so declensions do not chain except through the transforms named
  above.
- **Stem constraints**, each with a row that fails without it
  (`sources/mutate.py`, below):
  - stems are lowercase Greek letters (no space, apostrophe or digit); verb
    and adjective rules never take a capital, so `Ελληνικά`, `Πέτρα` and
    `Σάμη` at the start of a sentence are not read as the names Ελληνικό,
    Πέτρος and Σάμος before their own words;
  - a nominal ending without an accent needs the word's accent in the stem
    (`για`, `στα`, `μια` are not γιος, στο, μιας); consonant-only perfective
    endings need a two-letter stem (`έξω`, `άσε` are not έχω, άνω); the
    augmented imperfect needs a vowel (`ήταν` is not τω); the unaugmented
    stressed past needs a vowel before the ending and two syllables (`μέσα`
    is not μέσω); the -α imperative three letters (`ένα` is not ενώ); the
    -ά- endings of conjugation II a consonant before them (`ποιά` is not
    ποιώ); the accent moves left only for the forms the rules mark (`κυρία`
    is not κύρια);
  - exclusions: `μέρες` is not μέρη or μέρος, `αυτιά` is not αυτός;
  - left out: the archaic 3rd plural -άν (`χαράν` is not χαρώ; in the
    subtitle list `ζητάν`, `μιλάν` occur 257 times against 9,858 for the
    nouns `δωρεάν`, `σοβάν`), and ρ/ρν outside φέρνω and σούρνω (`γέρω` is
    not γέρνω).
- **History**: the first commit of this pack, `el: own rules`, has the own
  rules as written before Yomitan's file was read, and `el: port Yomitan's
  rules and tests` follows it. The later commits changed the own rules
  after measuring, each named for what it changed: the accent step (`el:
  own rules: the accent moved by a second step, without pattern steps`),
  the false candidates of the frequent words and the gaps of the UniMorph
  sample, lowercase stems for verbs and adjectives, elided function words,
  one-syllable words and older spellings.

## Known gaps

- **Untagged dictionary rows.** wty-el-en gives a part of speech only to
  verbs, so with the published dictionary a noun or adjective the rules
  reach is dropped by the part-of-speech filter; most inflected forms still
  have their own form-of row. `lookup.untaggedTerms: "pass"`
  (bee-san/hoshidicts#58) fixes it, and the `tagged` numbers above measure
  it; the pack sets the field once it is on main. Hachidori needs engines
  built after #58 to load the pack then.
- `gaps.tsv` (16 rows): irregular stems not in the tables (`εφεύραμε` →
  εφευρίσκω, `κατασχέσουν` → κατάσχω, `πόλαβε` → προλαβαίνω, `σύρουνε` →
  σέρνω), the variant lemmas ακούγω and καίγω (`άκουσα`, `έκαψε`, `κάψτε`),
  the archaic `ζητάν`, `αναγγέλλοντας` → αναγγέλω (UniMorph's spelling of
  the lemma), the capitalised `Σαββατοκύριακου`, `ματιάσματα` (the accent of
  μάτιασμα is two syllables back), and UniMorph's own errors (`είμαι` →
  ήμαστε, `έχοντας` → έχετε and είχαμε, `αμετάθετοε`).
- **First rank** stays at 68% on the sample for the dictionary's lemma
  conventions (Measurements 1).
- **Two trace steps** for a reading whose accent moves (`aorist (1st sg)`,
  `accent`), and a homograph shows the reading of the first transform
  (`άφησε`: aorist rather than imperative).
- **One hop** of form-of references: 37 of the 10,000 frequent words (0.21%
  of the tokens) show no card, as `μέρες` → μέρα → ημέρα.
- **Capitals**: `κυριακή` shows Κυριακή (Sunday), and a common word written
  with a capital can still reach a name the dictionary capitalises through
  the noun endings names take (`Βασιλείου` → Βασίλειος, tagged); names in
  other endings (`Δωδεκανησιών`) are not reached by rules.
- **Older text**: the katharevousa-tinged spelling of 19th-century prose
  (accusatives in -ν such as `κεφαλήν`, `ημέραν`; `ολίγον`) is not
  deinflected, and wty-el-en lists few such words. The older augment, the
  movable -ν and `είνε` are read.
- **No ranking signal** among readings of equal length and trace: wty-el-en
  has no frequencies, so `ορίζεσαι` lists ορίζω before ορίζομαι.
- `addMissingAccent` (setting `missingAccents`, off by default) adds one
  accent at the first or last place of each vowel letter, which covers most
  words but not every place; the rules read text in capitals without it.
