# Russian (`ru`)

Deinflection rules, text processors and scanning settings for Russian.
Lookups start at the hovered word and try only prefixes that end at a word
boundary (`word` / `word` / `spaces`, docs/languages.md 14.1), so `кто-то`,
`по-русски` and phrases the dictionary lists (`в самом деле`) are found
whole, and a word longer than the scan length is still looked up whole.

wiktionary-to-yomitan's Russian dictionaries leave the part-of-speech
(`rules`) column empty on every row (wty-ru-en: 0 of 1,493,389; wty-ru-ru: 0
of 3,379,353). By first definition tag, wty-ru-en's rows are 1,432,382
form-of rows (`non-lemma`), 28,581 `n`, 12,858 `v`, 9,944 `adj`, 4,930
`name`, 2,488 `adv` and 2,206 others. So the pack sets `lookup.untaggedTerms`
to `pass` (docs/languages.md 9.4): an untagged row matches every form whose
conditions name a dictionary form. Without it, a form a rule reaches
(`книгой` → `книга`) would match no row of the recommended dictionary.

## Rule provenance

`transforms.json` has 115 transforms and 4,576 rules, all `own`: Yomitan has
no Russian rules, so the own rules are also the union and the final set, and
`tools/merge-transforms.mjs ru` writes `transforms.json` from
`sources/own.json` alone. No rule was removed from it.

- **Rows.** Suffix rules of one transform that differ only in their endings
  are written as one cross-product row: a `circumfix` whose only prefix is
  empty, with the endings as its `suffixes` (docs/languages.md 7.3). The
  engine expands the 1,544 rows into the 4,576 rules when the pack loads, and
  the expanded rules give the same deinflections as separate suffix rules: a
  copy whose 232 cross-product rows are written out as their 3,259 suffix
  rules gives byte-identical `hoshidicts-cli deinflect ru --stdin` output on
  the fourth auditor's 219,661 inputs (802,502 results, order included) and
  the sixth auditor's 500,990 (3,422,059). The
  rows keep `tests/` and `sources/` under 1 MB (999,849 bytes) and
  `tests/classes.json` at 316,574 bytes.
- **Transforms name forms.** Rules whose ending stands for the same set of
  forms make one transform, and its name lists them, so a trace says every
  form an ending can be: `книги` is `genitive singular, nominative plural`,
  `красивого` is `genitive/accusative masculine, genitive neuter`, `читаю`
  is `first-person singular present/future`. Each description gives up to
  three examples of its rules.
- **What the rules cover**:
  - nouns: the masculine, neuter and feminine declensions with the spelling
    rules after г к х, ж ш щ ч and ц; -ий, -ия, -ие, -ья, -ье, -мя; the
    locative in -у; the fleeting vowel of the stem (`отца` → `отец`, `дня` →
    `день`, `любви` → `любовь`, `лаптя` → `лапоть`) and the vowel inserted in
    the genitive plural (`ошибок`, `сестёр`, `песен`, `окон`, `писем`);
    -анин/-янин, -ёнок, -ья plurals and `сыновья`, `друзья`; plural-only
    nouns (`часов` → `часы`); mother and daughter (`матери`, `дочерьми`) and
    the irregular plurals (`люди`, `дети`, `небеса`, `хозяева`);
  - adjectives: the long forms of the hard, soft, velar and hushing stems,
    -ий with ь (`третьего`, `лисьей`), and the same endings on nouns and
    surnames declined as adjectives (`учёного`, `столовой`, `животного`,
    `Толстого`); short forms with and without the fleeting vowel
    (`интересен`, `умён`, `короток`, `зол`); comparatives (`быстрее`,
    `громче`, `дороже`, `лучше`, `по-` comparatives such as `побыстрее`) and
    superlatives in -ейший/-айший; adverbs of manner (`по-старинному`,
    `по-дружески`);
  - pronouns and numerals, as whole words (`меня`, `него`, `кому-нибудь`,
    `этого`, `всех`, `чьих`, `двух`, `пятью`, `двухсот`);
  - verbs: the present and perfective future of every conjugation class with
    the consonant change of the first person (`хожу`, `люблю`, `плачу`,
    `пишу`, `могу`), the -ова-/-ева-, -ава- and -нуть classes, the stems that
    change (`жду`, `беру`, `живу`, `иду`, `несу`, `веду`, `умру`, `жму`,
    `собью`), and the irregular verbs (хотеть, бежать, дать, есть, взять,
    -нять, -чать, лечь, сесть, -честь, жечь, петь, слать); the past, the
    imperative, the four participles (declined and short: `читающего`,
    `сделана`, `решён`) and both adverbial participles; the reflexive -ся and
    -сь on every form.
- **Conditions.** `v`, `n`, `name`, `adj`, `pron` and `num` are dictionary
  forms. `fl` (a stem whose fleeting vowel is missing) and `cmp` (a
  comparative after its по- was removed) are steps no row carries, so `отц`
  and `быстрее` (from `побыстрее`) only feed the rule that completes them.
  Participle rules take an adjective's output (`читающего` → `читающий` →
  `читать`). Under `untaggedTerms: "pass"` an untagged row matches any of
  the six dictionary forms but never `fl` or `cmp` alone.
- **ё and е.** Russian text usually writes е for ё, and wiktionary-to-yomitan
  writes ё. Every ending with ё has a twin with е (`идёт`, `идет`), every
  long adjective ending also reads the stem's last е as ё (`темном` →
  `тёмный`, `веселого` → `весёлый`), and `yoToE` adds a variant with one е
  as ё (`перенес` → `перенёс`). Without the stem reading, the adverb in -о
  that a noun rule reaches without a processor step (`темно`, `весело`)
  came before the adjective.
- **Stem constraints.** Every rule keeps its stem within Cyrillic letters and
  the hyphen (`stemCharacters`), so an ending never fires on a short word
  after a space or a punctuation mark. Every regular ending needs a stem of
  two letters, or three for the zero ending of the genitive plural, so that
  short words are not read as forms of other short words; the verbs and
  nouns whose stem is shorter are written out (`дал`, `пил`, `жил`, `был`,
  `мыл`, `умер`, `тёр`, `рта`, `льда`). The measured collisions they remove:
  - `он`, `ты`, `мы` and `сам` were the genitive plurals of она/оно, т, мыть
    and с; `мало`, `сила` and `тело` were the pasts of мать, сить and теть;
    `мер` and `тел` were мереть and тло (`дело` stays the past of деть, which
    is written out, after the noun дело);
  - the imperative in -ь needs three letters (`весь` and `три` were весить
    and тереть, every plural in -и an imperative in -ичь);
  - -знавать is read only with ё (`узнаёт`): written with е, `узнает`,
    `знаешь` and `знает` are узнать and знать (56 such forms, 466,129
    occurrences in the subtitle list), which the regular rules read;
  - the ч, ж, ш and щ of the consonant-change classes need two letters before
    them (`счете` was скать);
  - the masculine past in -ёл and -ёс needs the letter of an -ести verb before
    it (`вёл`, `мёл`, `брёл`, `нёс`): `почел` was почести, and the -честь
    verbs are written out;
  - the short forms of -ой adjectives are written out, because a rule from -а,
    -о and -ы to -ой read nouns and adverbs as them (`дома` → домой, `пока`
    → покой).
- **Capitalised words.** A word at the start of a sentence is looked up as
  written and, one processor step later, in lower case; the comparator ranks
  fewer steps first (docs/languages.md 9.6), so a reading of the capitalised
  word that lands on a name came first. Zero endings, comparatives,
  superlatives and -ой adjectives leave out stems that start with a capital,
  as no name takes them: `Итак` was Итака, `Вас` Вася, `Лет` Лета, `Наше`
  Наха, `Милая` Мила and `Никого` Ника (35 positions in the three texts; 0
  now). The surnames in -ой keep their oblique forms through wty-ru-en's own
  form-of rows (`Толстого` → `Толстой`, `tests/lookup.tsv`), not through the
  rules; the other adjectival surnames keep their rules (`Достоевского` →
  `Достоевский`).
- **History.** The first commit of this pack, `ru: own rules`, has the own
  rules as written before Yomitan's Russian files were read (109 transforms,
  3,367 rules). Later commits changed them after measuring:
  - processors: once Yomitan's were read, `removeStressMarks` became
    `removeRussianDiacritics`, and `eForYo` and `yoForE` became one `yoToE`,
    so that check-yomitan pairs them; the rules did not change;
  - building the corpus added adjectival nouns and surnames (`учёного`,
    `запятые`), declined and short participles (`читающего`, `сделана`),
    -уемый, the inserted о of prefixed verbs (`собью`), жать, -олоть and
    -ороть, прясть, сыпать, prefixed есть, the past adverbial participles in
    -ши, and the plurals of -ярин and -арин;
  - the collisions of the 10,000 frequent words and the three texts gave the
    stem constraints, the capital exclusions and the е → ё stem reading
    above, and the -честь verbs;
  - adverbs of manner with по- were added after the hand-judged sample of
    the texts (`по-старинному`), and the -стичь verbs after the corpus
    (`достигнет` → достичь, not only достигнуть);
  - the search for the mutation check's rows and a list of fleeting-vowel
    nouns found the rest: `колец`, `крылец`, `тюрем`, `свадеб`, `денег`,
    `ковра`, `кашля`, `свёкра`, `секвойе`, `карими`, `остёр`, `господу`.

  rule outputs never match wiktionary-to-yomitan's Russian rows, and lookups
  reach a lemma only through the form-of rows of the word as written.
- **One word at a time.** Rules change the last word of a phrase only and
  never cross a space (`stemCharacters`), so phrases the dictionary lists in
  another form are not reached (`так называемые` → так называемый), nor are
  compounds whose two parts both inflect (`государства-доноры`). The
  analytic future (`буду читать`) is read as буду → быть and читать
  separately, unless the dictionary lists the phrase.
- **Not deinflected by rules**: diminutives, augmentatives and other
  derivations (`собачка`), aspect pairs (`прочитать` and `читать`) and
  -ость nouns. wiktionary-to-yomitan's form-of rows name their base words
  (`собачка` → собака, "diminutive"), and lookups follow them.
- **Writing forms out.** Verbs and nouns whose stem has one letter are
  written out, with their prefixed verbs by suffix rules (`дал`, `отдала`,
  `пил`, `допил`); a short stem that the lists miss is not reached through a
  regular ending. The fleeting vowel is written as patterns of the letters
  around it, with the few nouns that fit no pattern written out (`рот`,
  `лоб`, `лёд`, `ложь`).
- **Tests that cannot see a setting.** `traceSelection` changes no first
  result of the tests. The negative `душа` ↛ душить keeps out a real but
  rare reading (душа́, the adverbial participle of душить), which
  `gerExclude: 'душ'` removes on purpose.
- **Homographs** are listed in the order the ranking keys leave them
  (docs/languages.md 9.6): `стали` (стать, сталь), `дома` (дома, дом), `тем`
  (тем, тот, тьма), `уже` (уже, уж), `для` (для, длить), `три` (три, тереть).
  The rule order decides these, which are both readings in real text (the
  third auditor's Tatoeba samples): `начала` (начать, начал, then начало; 11
  verb and 19 noun of 30), `дорог` (дорогой, then дорога; 10 and 19 of 29),
  `еду` (ехать, then еда; 15 and 15 of 30), `передней`, `переднюю`
  (передний, then передняя), `плачу` (плакать, then платить) and `мочи`
  (моча, мочь, мочить).
