# Ukrainian (`uk`)

Deinflection rules, text processors and scanning settings for Ukrainian.
Lookups start at the hovered word and try only prefixes that end at a word
boundary (`word` / `word` / `spaces`, docs/languages.md 14.1). The
apostrophe is part of a word (`wordInternal`: ', ’, ʼ, ‘, `, ´), so м’ясо and
п’ять are looked up whole; hyphens separate words, so будь-якого is looked
up whole and from якого.

The processors are Yomitan's: decapitalisation, capitalisation of the first
letter, stress marks removed (the acute, and here also the grave), and the
apostrophe variants (', ’ and ʼ tried for any of the six). One processor is
this pack's own: `alternateInitialUV` tries a word's initial у before a
consonant as в and the other way round (учитель, вчитель; Вкраїні, Україні),
the alternation the spelling rules allow and texts use. No processor or
transform is optional, so every test file runs the same with every option on
and with the pack's defaults.

## Rule provenance

`transforms.json` is written by `node languages/uk/sources/build-final.mjs`:
the union `tools/merge-transforms.mjs` makes of `sources/own.json` and
`sources/yomitan.json`, with the own transforms first, every Yomitan rule
limited to one word (`stemCharacters` `{stem}`, as the own rules are: Yomitan's
rules have no stem constraint and read на столі as на стіл), and the
Yomitan rules `sources/yomitan-removed.tsv` lists left out.

- 113 transforms: 79 own, 34 Yomitan (Yomitan's 35 without `superlative`,
  whose two `най-` prefix rules are left out; the own superlative rules go
  to the positive in one step).
- 8,529 rules: 4,645 own, 490 both, 3,394 Yomitan. By type: 5,968 suffix,
  2,492 whole-word and 69 circumfix (prefix and suffix) rules, written as 657
  JSON rules because rules that differ only in their affixes are one cross
  product (section 7.3; a whole word is a cross product whose stem is empty,
  `stemCharacters` `[]`). No `pattern` rule: Yomitan's 64 non-literal rules
  map to suffix rules with `stemChange` (the о/е → і alternation),
  `stemEndsWith` (fleeting vowels), `stemContains` (the zero ending) and
  literal palatalised endings.
- `both`: 1,257 of the 5,135 own rules make a replacement one of Yomitan's
  rules makes (the same affixes and stem change, an overlapping part of
  speech). 490 of them are in cross products all of whose pairs Yomitan
  makes and are marked `both`; the other 767 sit in own cross products with
  pairs Yomitan lacks and keep the mark `own`, so the rules stay in the order
  `build-own.mjs` gives them (rules matching at one place apply in file
  order, which decides ties, section 9.6).
- **Yomitan rules left out**: the 155 of `sources/yomitan-removed.tsv`,
  each named by its transform, affixes, constraints and conditions, chosen by
  `sources/select-removals.py` from `eval-language --per-rule` on the
  measurement set: every form of the kaikki tables (311,298 rows, written by
  `build-corpus.mjs --all`), `tests/cases.tsv` and Yomitan's test with any
  trace. A rule is left out when it breaks a negative, when it adds false
  candidates and recovers no row, or when it adds at least 5 false candidates
  and more than three per row it recovers:
  - 26 break 35 negatives, such as `imperative` `й` → `ти` (білий → білити,
    новий → новити, любий → любити, старий → старити), `active participle`
    `лий` → `ти` (малий → мати, милий → мити), `genitive` `ого` → `ий`
    (того → тий, його → йий), `present` `у` → `ати` (вужчу → вужчати,
    сильнішу → сильнішати, живу → живати), `superlative` `най-` (найближча
    → ближчий, where the own rules give близький) and `genitive` `ю` →
    nothing (бою → бо; it also adds 539 false candidates: даю → да, морю →
    мор, синю → син);
  - 26 add 1,971 false candidates and recover 110 rows, such as `passive
    participle` `тий` → `ти` and `ний` → `ти` (448 and 447 for 17 and 16
    rows) and `truncated stem` `а` → `ати` (100: кара → карати, літа →
    літати; it recovers зна → знати and бува → бувати of Yomitan's test);
  - 103 recover nothing and add 1,408 false candidates, such as `present`
    `у` → `ити` (491: буду → будити, живу → живити, любу → любити) and
    `active participle` `ачий` → `ати` (323).

  Together they are the only rules for 199 rows and add 4,897 false
  candidates. Measurements 1 and 4 compare the final set with the union.
- **Own rules**: every rule's stem is limited to one word (`{stem}`: letters,
  the apostrophes and the hyphen), so не знаю is not не знати.
  - Nouns: the endings of the four declensions with the hard, soft and mixed
    groups and the consonant before them (`stemEndsWith`), the і ~ о/е
    alternation (стола → стіл, ночі → ніч, ніг → нога, льоду → лід),
    fleeting vowels (будинку, хлопця, учня, пальця, рубля, вітру, сну, пса,
    книжок, сестер, пісень, вікон), the second palatalisation (нозі, руці,

    діти, очі, друзі, the 1928 genitive -ости (радости).
  - Adjectives in -ий and -ій with every case form, which also reach nouns,
    pronouns and participles declined as adjectives (хворого, якого,
    прочитаного: wiktionary-to-yomitan files participles under the verb),
    comparatives (новіший, вищий, нижчий, легший, товщий, кращий) and

    (повен, рад), possessive adjectives (батькового, сестриною; without -ова,
    -ових and -овими, which are also the relational adjectives in -овий:
    світова is not світів), indefinite pronouns (якогось, якого-небудь).
  - Pronouns and numerals as whole words (мене, нього, моєї, цього, всіх,

    хто and що (кого-небудь, когось, будь-кого) and сей, оцей.
  - Verbs: the present of both conjugations by stem class (-ати, -яти, -іти,
    -увати, -авати, -нути, -ити, -сти, -зти, -кти, -гти, -ерти, -рати, -бти
    and the irregular бути, дати, їсти, -вісти, іти, йти, їхати, хотіти,
    спати, взяти, -йняти), the mutations of the 1st person singular (ходжу,
    вожу, ношу, плачу, люблю, роблю), the -ати verbs that mutate through the
    present (пишу, кажу, плачу, шепчу, брешу), the synthetic future
    (читатиму), the past (читав, ніс, віз, міг, вів, зник, пішов), the
    imperative (читай, пиши, сядь, вибач, кинь, пий), passive and active
    participles, the impersonal -но/-то, the adverbial participles, the
    reflexive -ся/-сь and -ться on every form, the infinitive in -ть and
    -тись.
  - Stem constraints and order, each from a measurement:
    - a one-letter noun ending needs two letters of stem, so на is not read
      as a form of но, nor ті as a form of ть;
    - the second-conjugation -ати class is written after ж, ч and щ; before
      -у, -им and -ім, which comparative adjectives also end in, ч after ж
      is excluded, so дорожчим is not дорожчати while бряжчить is
      бряжчати;
    - іти keeps its і only bare or after п (піду, пішла), so дідуся is not
      дітися;
    - -авати comes before -ати after д and т (дає, продає, стає, видає:
      давати, продавати, ставати, видавати first) and after it after н and
      р (знає: знати before знавати); -іяти comes before -іти (мрію: мріяти
      before мріти); the own transforms come before Yomitan's (їм: вони
      before їсти; both readings are common: of the 253 lookups of їм
      after a function word in Tatoeba, labelled one by one in review, 142
      mean вони and 111 їсти, which comes second).
  - Frequent homographs: when the rules reach two lemmas of a word in one
    step each and the word's rows name both, the term found first comes
    first (docs/languages.md 9.6), and for many frequent words that was the
    rarer one (року → рок, голові → гол, погані → погань, од → ода). 206
    whole-word rules, found before any suffix rule, put the lemma a reader
    means first: року → рік, голові → голова, погані → поганий, банку →
    банк, добра → добрий, заплачу → заплатити, шию → шия, статей → стаття,
    тлі → тло, осі → вісь, методи → метод, на ході → хід, в вічі → око,
    трупах → труп; Львові → Львів has an empty `conditionsOut`, since
    wty-uk-en gives names no part of speech. The words are the hand-labelled
    frequent words of both frequency lists (Measure notes) whose first
    result was the other lemma, the words of the measurements' wrong lists
    whose contexts show the same, the other table forms of those lemmas
    that had the same problem, and the words the reviews' context samples
    decided (банку, назву, складу, мила, умов, заклади, мешканці).
  - Plurals in -ці: the rule -ці → -ець (українці → українець) is written
    with the letter before -ці (-нці → -нець), so it is found before the
    two-letter -ці → -ка of the feminine (українка). It changes the first
    result of 27 words, all to the plural's -ець (мешканці, українці,
    іспанці, канадці, іноземці, вегетаріанці), which running text means
    at nearly every occurrence; марці keeps марка (a whole-word rule), and
    -лець nouns, whose plural is -льці (пальці), are left out. Where the contexts favour the reading the engine
    already gives first, there is no rule (уяви: уява, зміни: зміна). The
    other lemma stays in the list. Two have transforms of their own:
    од → від (`alt.od`, the older form of the preposition, 430 times in
    Kotsiubynsky) and видимо → видимий (`adv.pos`). Putting the adjective
    transforms before the noun transforms as well (tried with the first 70
    of these rules) changes the first result of 25 labelled words: 12
    become wrong (другу → другий, дорогу → дорогий, тепла → теплий) and
    none becomes right.

   a lemma the hovered word means.

     in `sources/homographs.tsv` when it has one: the label lists the lemma
     a reader most likely means, or several only when each is common in
     context (права: правий, право; їм: вони, їсти; золота: золотий, золото,
     since the subtitles behind uk_50k likely mean the noun). 604 words were
     labelled: the 366 frequent words of this kind (the 10,000 most frequent
     and the words used 15 times or more in the texts), those of the
     Wikipedia list's 10,000 that the proxy misjudged, the words of the
     homograph rules, and words of the wrong lists and the reviews' samples
     judged in their contexts; the file keeps the 198 labels that change a
     verdict.
   - Without a label, only the named lemma most frequent in `uk_50k.txt`
     counts, or in the Wikipedia list when `uk_50k.txt` has none of them (in
     words mode the measured list comes first), a verb's doublets in -нути
     and -ти as one: a frequency proxy, which Measurements 4 checks by hand
     (it misses about one tie in ten). When neither list has any of them,
     the lookup is a tie, counted apart and judged by hand (names, mostly).
   - Otherwise the word itself (a headword with definitions), a lemma one
     of the word's form-of rows names, or a lemma the rows of that lemma
     name as another spelling or aspect (вмію → вміти → уміти) is right; a
     link that makes it an inflected form of another lemma (мило, also a
     past form of мити) does not count. A capitalised word at the start of a
     sentence is judged by the lowercase word's rows when it has any, so
     Мені → Мена (a town) and Я → Я (the letter's entry, tagged `char`) are
     wrong (only a capital letter's entry; Із → з is right); a capitalised
     entry of the same spelling that is not a letter (Сонце, Ви) is judged
     as before; a comparative in -ше, -ще
     or -че is also the adverb's (міцніше: міцно). The rows of мене, тобі,
     нього and the other personal pronouns in wty-uk-en name every personal
     pronoun, so for them the lemma comes from a list in the script.

## Known gaps

- The 12 sampled rows of `gaps.tsv`: hyphenated compounds whose two parts
  decline (балу-маскарадові → бал-маскарад, краваткам-метеликам →
  краватка-метелик); table forms filed under a noun (асфальтує, скрипім);
  nouns declined as adjectives whose lemma is the feminine or the plural
  (убогим → убога, нежуйними → нежуйні); забито → впісятись (a wrong row of
  Wiktionary's table); мерцеві → мрець (the е the oblique forms add);
  клепли (a dialect imperative); няньовім → нянів (ь before the ending of
  the -ів possessive); штирма and їдна (dialect numerals).
- Valid readings left out on purpose, so that a frequent word is not read
  as a rare one: бряжчу (бряжчати; -жчу is excluded because comparatives
  end in it, дорожчу), єрі (єрь: the third-declension rule wants д, т, з,
  с, ц, л or н before the ending), both rows of `gaps.tsv`; мить is not
  the infinitive in -ть of мити, and коли is not the imperative of колоти
  (negatives in `cases.tsv`).
- Ties the homograph rules do not decide are left in the engine's order
  (section 9.6): on a hand-judged sample about one in ten lookups of an
  unlabelled ambiguous word gets the other reading first (на лови → ловити,
  тепла хвиля → тепло, луки та оточили → лука; Measurements 4). вдає gives
  удати before удавати (5 places in Tatoeba): both are reached by a rule
  and a form-of reference (вдати → удати, вдавати → удавати), and the
  homographs and doublets of Measurements 1 tie the same way (йогам → йога
  before йог). Choosing between them by frequency would need the engine.
- Names whose forms tie are left in the engine's order: Мирослави,
  Мирославу and Мирославі show Мирослав first for the heroine Мирослава of
  *Захар Беркут* (43 lookups), Іванові the possessive Іванів for the dative
  of Іван (10 in Kotsiubynsky), and Атен the goddess Атена (an alternative
  of Афіна) for Атени, Athens (5 in Tatoeba).
- Words whose reading depends on the genre keep the order the subtitles and
  Tatoeba favour: друга and другу show друг, where Wikipedia and the texts
  mean другий (29 of the 35 text lookups); переклади and склади the
  imperative, where Wikipedia means the noun; лови ловити, where the texts
  mean the hunt (на лови); купи купити (17 of 20 Tatoeba sentences), where
  the texts mean купа (32 of 33); долі доля, where the texts mostly mean
  the adverb (down, 13 of 15 read); дію, ліг (ліга in Wikipedia), танків and
  танки (танок, a dance, in the texts) and голу (гол, a goal, in football
  articles) split the same way. `measure.py` counts these by their labels,
  which list both readings, or by the proxy, which follows the subtitles;
  the counts here are by hand.
